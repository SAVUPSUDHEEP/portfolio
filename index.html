About Me

The Story So Far

I'm Savu P. Sudheep, an AI & Machine Learning graduate from Kerala, India. My journey has taken me from building intelligent systems in classrooms to deploying real-world AI solutions in Japan.

I'm passionate about Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and building technology that genuinely helps people — like my stray dog rescue system that combines YOLOv8, CNNs, and a Flutter app into one cohesive platform.

Japan changed me. Six months as an exchange student at Shimane University and as an AI Developer Intern at Sanres Foods Corporation — those experiences shaped my worldview, my work ethic, and my ambition.

I speak English, Malayalam, Hindi, and Japanese (JLPT N5 certified, working toward N4). I'm actively seeking opportunities in Japan or international tech environments.
